En resumen: Lucky 8 no tiene cuentas de usuario y no envía a nuestros servidores nada de lo que anotas. Tu configuración y tus resultados se guardan solo en tu teléfono. Los anuncios los muestra Google AdMob y los pagos los procesa Google Play; cada uno se rige por las políticas de Google.

Lucky 8 es una aplicación desarrollada en Colombia. Para cualquier pregunta sobre privacidad, escríbenos a christianduc.trd@gmail.com.

Lucky 8 es una herramienta educativa para juegos de casino. Sirve para llevar el registro de la mesa, ver estadísticas y calcular pagos y probabilidades. No pedimos tu nombre, correo, teléfono ni ubicación. No creamos cuentas, no leemos tus contactos y no vendemos datos personales.

Bloqueamos los anuncios de apuestas y casinos. Puedes restablecer o desactivar el identificador de publicidad en los ajustes de Android (Google › Anuncios). Más información: cómo usa Google los datos en los anuncios.

Lucky 8 · Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

In short: Lucky 8 has no user accounts and does not send anything you record to our servers. Your settings and results are stored only on your phone. Ads are shown by Google AdMob and payments are processed by Google Play, each under Google's policies.

1. Who is responsible

Lucky 8 is an app developed in Colombia. For any privacy question, write to christianduc.trd@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

Lucky 8 is an educational tool for casino games. It helps you keep track of the table, see statistics and work out payouts and odds. It does not handle real money: you can't bet with it, it doesn't take or pay bets, and it isn't connected to any casino. It does not guarantee winnings. It is for adults 18+ only.

3. Data stored on your phone

  • Your settings: language, currency, chips, colors, designs and preferences.
  • What you record in each game: results, cards, dice and numbers.
  • A background photo, if you choose one. A reduced copy is kept only inside the app.
  • The status of your full version and of the daily videos.

This data never leaves your phone and we have no access to it. It is deleted if you use «Reset all» in Settings or uninstall the app.

4. Data we do not collect

We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or location. We don't create accounts, read your contacts or sell personal data.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a small fixed ad at the bottom of the screen, and you can watch videos with ads to unlock features for a while. The full version has no ads. These ads are shown by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, technical device information and your approximate location (from your IP address) to show and measure ads. We block gambling and casino ads. You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in Android settings (Google › Ads). More: how Google uses data in ads.

6. Purchases and subscriptions (Google Play)

Payments for the full version (24-hour pass and subscriptions) are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details. You can cancel a subscription anytime in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. See Google's privacy policy.

7. Permissions

The app only accesses your photos if you choose a background photo, and only the photo you select.

8. Minors

Lucky 8 is for adults 18+ only and is not directed at minors. If you are under 18, do not use the app.

9. Security

Because your data is stored only on your phone, its security also depends on your device's protection (screen lock and updates).

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with its update date.

11. Contact
